About Me

Thank you for visiting my web site! This page is here to help you get to know me a little better.


I am a dependable and detail-oriented candidate seeking opportunities to gain practical experience, with strong organizational skills, attention to detail, and a commitment to continued learning and growth in software development.


I have been interested in programming from a young age, after being introduced to the field through the 1983 film WarGames. What began as a childhood curiosity quickly developed into a lasting interest, leading me to continuously build my knowledge and technical skills since high school. While actual programming is significantly different from what Hollywood makes it out to be, the challenge and problem-solving involved have only strengthened my enthusiasm for the field.


The first program I developed was a simple clicker game for my high school computer science course’s final project. Creating a project from the ground up and seeing it function in real time provided a strong sense of accomplishment and helped shape my academic development. Since then, I have continued to build a diverse technical foundation through my coursework at UCLA and hands-on experience, strengthening my problem-solving abilities and technical skills. I am a quick learner, a strong team player, and a highly motivated individual.
